# Changelog

All notable changes to this application are documented in this file.

## 0.1.0

- Consents become CONSENT_ attributes on Brevo contacts, created when first needed
- Lists with "Sync met Brevo" get a Brevo list; joining and leaving follow right away
- Nightly full sync of synced lists and consents
- Unsubscribes in Brevo withdraw consents and list memberships in Twenty
